# 06 — Sales playbook

Use these scripts as starting points; adjust tone to the seller. Placeholders are in `{braces}`. Never send more than one follow-up to someone who has not replied.

## Who to contact

A good prospect:

- Runs Facebook ads for COD products (check the page's ad library or recent sponsored posts), or has a WooCommerce/Shopify store with COD enabled.
- Posts frequently and has active comment sections (a sign of 300+ orders/month).
- Mentions Steadfast, Pathao or RedX, or shows parcel photos.

## Cold messages

### First DM — Bangla

> আসসালামু আলাইকুম। আপনার পেজটা দেখলাম, প্রোডাক্টগুলো দারুণ। একটা ছোট প্রশ্ন: মাসে মোটামুটি কতগুলো পার্সেল রিটার্ন আসে, আন্দাজ করে বলতে পারবেন?
>
> আমরা "Shipnod" নামে একটা টুল বানিয়েছি। অর্ডার শিপ করার আগেই এটা কাস্টমারের ডেলিভারি হিস্ট্রি চেক করে, সন্দেহজনক অর্ডার SMS-এ কাস্টমারকে দিয়ে কনফার্ম করায়, আর এক ক্লিকে Steadfast/Pathao/RedX-এ বুক করে দেয়।
>
> কয়েকজন সেলারের সাথে ১৪ দিনের ফ্রি পাইলট করছি। রিটার্ন না কমলে কোনো টাকা লাগবে না। ১০ মিনিট কথা বলা যাবে?
>
> — {আপনার নাম}, Shipnod

### First DM — English

> Hi {name}, I came across your page and really like your products. Quick question: roughly how many parcels come back to you each month?
>
> We built Shipnod, a tool that checks a customer's delivery history before you ship, gets doubtful orders confirmed by the customer over SMS, and books Steadfast/Pathao/RedX in one click.
>
> We're running a free 14-day pilot with a few sellers. If your returns don't go down, you pay nothing. Do you have 10 minutes for a quick call?
>
> — {your name}, Shipnod

### Follow-up (once, 2–3 days later) — Bangla

> আগের মেসেজটা হয়তো দেখার সময় পাননি। ছোট একটা হিসাব: মাসে ১,০০০ অর্ডারে ২৫% রিটার্ন হলে, প্রতি রিটার্নে ১৫০ টাকা ধরলে মাসে প্রায় ৩৭,৫০০ টাকা লস হয়। আপনার পেজের জন্য হিসাবটা ফ্রিতে করে দেখাতে পারি। আগ্রহী হলে শুধু "হ্যাঁ" লিখুন।

### Follow-up — English

> Following up once. A quick calculation: with 1,000 orders a month and 25% returns, at about 150 taka per return, that is roughly 37,500 taka lost every month. Happy to run the numbers for your page for free. Just reply "yes" if you're interested.

### Facebook group post — Bangla (value first, follow group rules)

> রিটার্ন পার্সেলে মাসে কত টাকা যাচ্ছে, কখনও হিসাব করেছেন?
>
> একটা রিটার্নে খরচ: ডেলিভারি চার্জ + রিটার্ন চার্জ + প্যাকেজিং + যে অ্যাডের টাকায় অর্ডারটা এসেছিল। আমাদের সাথে কথা বলা সেলারদের হিসাবে এটা প্রায় ১২০–২৫০ টাকা।
>
> হিসাব: (মাসিক অর্ডার) × (রিটার্ন %) × (প্রতি রিটার্নের খরচ)
> উদাহরণ: ১,০০০ × ২৫% × ১৫০ = ৩৭,৫০০ টাকা/মাস।
>
> আপনার সংখ্যা কমেন্টে দিন, হিসাব করে দিচ্ছি। আর কীভাবে আমরা শিপ করার আগেই ফেক অর্ডার ধরি, কেউ জানতে চাইলে বলবেন।

Only state "sellers we spoke with estimate 120–250 taka" after the week-1 interviews actually support it; otherwise say "আমাদের ধারণা" (our estimate).

## 15-minute demo script

| Time | Step | What to do |
|------|------|------------|
| 0:00–3:00 | **Discovery** | Ask the discovery questions below. Write down their numbers. |
| 3:00–5:00 | **Cost of returns** | Calculate their monthly loss with their numbers: orders × RTO % × cost per return. Say the number out loud and let it sit. |
| 5:00–11:00 | **Live demo** | (1) Check the phone number of a customer who recently returned a parcel to them: show the score and reasons. (2) Create a test order with their own phone number: they receive the confirmation SMS and tap confirm. (3) Show one-click courier booking. (4) Show the dashboard's RTO and "money saved" view. |
| 11:00–13:00 | **Pilot offer** | 14-day free pilot, measured against their own baseline; first paid month is money-back if returns don't drop. Recommend a plan based on their order volume. |
| 13:00–15:00 | **Next steps** | Book a 30-minute setup slot within 48 hours. List what they need to bring (courier API credentials, store access, recent order outcomes). |

## Discovery questions

1. How many orders do you get per month? Through which channels (page, website, WhatsApp)?
2. Which couriers do you use? Inside vs outside Dhaka split?
3. Roughly what percentage of parcels come back? How do you know (courier panel, your own sheet, guess)?
4. What does one returned parcel cost you, including the ad that brought the order?
5. Who confirms orders today? How many hours a day does that take? What does that person cost?
6. Do you ask for an advance payment from some customers? How do you decide who?
7. What tools do you already use for orders and couriers?
8. Who else decides on buying a tool like this?
9. How would you prefer to pay: bKash, Nagad or card?

## Objection handling

| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| **"It's too expensive."** | "Starter is 990 taka a month. At about 150 taka per return, it pays for itself if it stops roughly 7 returned parcels a month. In the pilot you'll see the exact number before paying anything." |
| **"I already call every customer myself."** | "Calling works, but it costs hours every day, and most of those customers were going to accept anyway. Shipnod lets confirmed and low-risk customers go through on their own and gives your caller a short list of only the risky ones. Your caller stays; they just stop wasting time." |
| **"My courier already shows a success rate."** | "That check is useful, but it is typically one courier's data and someone has to look it up by hand for every order. Shipnod checks every order automatically, combines delivery outcomes across the sellers on our network with signals from the order itself, and then acts: confirmation SMS, advance-charge request or hold, before anything is booked." |
| **"Is my customer data safe? Will other sellers see my customers?"** | "No seller sees another seller's customers or orders. The shared network stores only a scrambled (hashed) version of phone numbers and how many parcels were delivered or returned, never names, addresses or what was ordered. You can ask us to delete your data at any time; it's in our privacy policy." |
| **"Customers will get annoyed by SMS."** | "Low-risk customers don't get an SMS at all. The others get one short message with a confirm button, which also stops 'I never ordered this' disputes at the door." |
| **"I'll think about it."** | "Of course. The pilot is free and setup takes about 30 minutes. Shall we start it now and decide after 14 days with real numbers?" |
| **"We use a different courier."** | "Which one? We support Steadfast, Pathao and RedX today; tell us which you need and we'll prioritise it. You can still use the risk checks and confirmations in the meantime." |

## Pilot offer

- **14-day free pilot.** No card needed.
- **Baseline:** the seller's RTO rate over the 30 days before the pilot (from their courier panel or order sheet). Agree on the number in writing at the start.
- **Money-back guarantee:** if the seller's RTO rate in the first paid month is not lower than the agreed baseline, refund that month in full.
- **Setup fee:** waived for the first 10 pilot sellers in exchange for a case study and testimonial (with their permission). After that, onboarding is 3,000–5,000 BDT depending on integrations.
- **Plan recommendation by volume:** up to 500 orders → Starter; up to 2,000 → Growth; up to 6,000 → Scale.

## Onboarding checklist

- [ ] Account created; API key issued; plan selected
- [ ] Baseline RTO agreed and recorded
- [ ] Courier credentials connected (Steadfast API key + secret, Pathao client credentials, RedX access token); test booking done
- [ ] Store connected (WooCommerce/Shopify webhook) or API/manual flow explained
- [ ] Last 90 days of order outcomes imported (if available)
- [ ] Message template language chosen (Bangla / Banglish / English); shop name set
- [ ] Cost per failed order set for "money saved" (default 150 BDT)
- [ ] Risk thresholds reviewed (defaults: <30 ship, 30–59 confirm, 60–79 advance, ≥80 hold)
- [ ] End-to-end test order with the seller's own phone number
- [ ] Staff shown how to handle held orders and no-reply orders
- [ ] Day-7 check-in booked
- [ ] Day-14 pilot review booked (results vs baseline, plan, payment method)
