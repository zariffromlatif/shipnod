# 05 — Go-to-market: the first 90 days

## Principle

Sell before scaling. The first customers come from **founder-led sales**: direct conversations with sellers, live demos with their own numbers, and a concierge pilot. Content, partners and the free checker grow the pipeline after the pitch has been proven in conversations.

## Channels

| Channel | How we use it | Cost | When |
|---------|---------------|------|------|
| **Direct DMs to Facebook-page sellers** | Personal Messenger/WhatsApp messages to pages that run COD ads. Scripts in [06-sales-playbook.md](06-sales-playbook.md). | Time | Week 2 onward |
| **Bangla Facebook groups** | Value-first posts in seller communities such as the **WE – Women and e-Commerce forum** and other e-commerce entrepreneur groups: an RTO cost calculator, "how we cut returns" breakdowns, case studies. Follow each group's rules; no spam. | Time | Week 3 onward |
| **Free "Customer Checker"** (lead magnet) | Free plan: 100 risk checks/month after sign-up. Every check is a reminder of the paid automation. Rate-limited; aggregate results only. | Low | Week 4 onward |
| **YouTube / Facebook reels in Bangla** | Short videos: "What a returned parcel really costs you", "3 signs an order is fake", demo walkthroughs. | Time | Week 7 onward |
| **WooCommerce / Shopify agencies and freelancers** | Reseller program: **20% recurring commission** for referred paying sellers, plus a share of setup fees if they do the integration. | Commission | Week 7 onward |
| **Courier merchant support / key account managers** | Couriers also lose money on returns. Offer KAMs a tool they can recommend to merchants with high RTO. | Time | Week 9 onward |
| **F-commerce trainers and course creators** | Sponsored segment or affiliate deal with trainers who teach Facebook selling. | Commission | Week 9 onward |
| **Customer referrals** | One month free for each referred seller who pays. | Discount | Week 9 onward |

## Funnel math (assumptions)

| Stage | Count | Conversion |
|-------|------:|-----------:|
| Targeted DMs sent | 300 | |
| Replies | 60 | 20% |
| Demos held | 25 | ~40% of replies |
| Pilots started | 10 | 40% of demos |
| Paying after pilot | 6 | 60% of pilots |

That is **about 2% from DM to paying seller**. Reaching the month-3 target of 40–60 paying sellers from DMs alone would take roughly 2,000–3,000 DMs (about 250 per week, ~35 per day). That is possible solo but heavy, which is why groups, the free checker, partners and referrals must start contributing from week 7. Replace every rate above with real numbers after the first 100 DMs.

## Week-by-week plan

| Week | Focus | Concrete outputs | Exit check |
|------|-------|------------------|------------|
| 1 | **Problem validation** | 30 seller interviews (phone, Messenger, in person). Collect: orders/month, couriers, RTO %, cost per return, who confirms orders. Build a list of 300 target pages/stores. Simple landing page. | At least 15 of 30 sellers say returns are a top-3 problem and can estimate the cost. |
| 2 | **Concierge MVP** | Run risk checks and confirmation SMS for 3 friendly sellers by hand (spreadsheet + SMS provider + v0.1 API). Measure their baseline RTO. | Process works end to end for 3 sellers. |
| 3 | **Outreach sprint 1** | 150 DMs, first group post, 10+ demos. | 3+ pilots agreed. |
| 4 | **Outreach sprint 2 + first revenue** | 150 more DMs, 10+ demos, Free checker live. Start charging setup fees to non-pilot customers. | 10 pilots running; first payment received. |
| 5 | **Pilot conversion** | Day-7 check-ins with each pilot; fix the top 3 issues. | Pilots actively using it (orders flowing daily). |
| 6 | **Day-45 decision** | Pilot reviews against baseline RTO; convert to paid; write case study #1 (with permission). | **Kill check:** at least 5 paying pilots, and measurable RTO reduction. Otherwise follow the pivot criteria in [02-business-plan.md](02-business-plan.md). |
| 7 | **Content engine** | 3 Bangla reels/week; 2 group posts/week; RTO calculator post. | Inbound leads start arriving. |
| 8 | **Partners** | Sign 5 WooCommerce/Shopify agencies or freelancers to the reseller program. | First partner-referred demo. |
| 9 | **Shopify + referrals** | Shopify flow polished; referral program live; first trainer partnership. | |
| 10 | **Courier relationships** | Meet merchant-support/KAM teams at the couriers; ask for official API guidance and introductions to high-RTO merchants. | At least 1 courier contact who will refer. |
| 11 | **Pricing and annual** | Review plan mix and ARPU; test annual prepay (2 months free) with happiest customers; test per-order pricing with 2–3 prospects. | |
| 12 | **Month-3 review** | Compare paying sellers, MRR, churn and RTO results with targets (40–60 paying). Plan v0.3. | Decide: push harder, adjust segment, or pivot. |
| 13 | **Retrospective** | Write down what worked, kill channels that do not convert, double the ones that do. | |

## Weekly metrics

Track these every week in one sheet.

| Area | Metric |
|------|--------|
| Pipeline | DMs sent, reply rate, demos held, pilots started |
| Revenue | Pilot-to-paid conversion, new paying sellers, MRR, setup-fee revenue |
| Retention | Logo churn, revenue churn, sellers inactive for 7+ days |
| Product usage | Orders processed, % of orders auto-scored, confirmation SMS sent, confirmation response rate |
| Value delivered | RTO rate per seller vs baseline, estimated money saved per seller |
| Cost | SMS cost per order, infra cost |
| Qualitative | Top 3 complaints, top 3 feature requests, quotes for case studies |

## Messaging pillars

1. **Money first:** "A returned parcel costs you around 150 taka. Nishchit stops many of them before they leave your shop." (Use the seller's own number in demos.)
2. **Less calling:** "Your staff only call the orders that need a call."
3. **Works with what you use:** Steadfast, Pathao, RedX, WooCommerce, Shopify, Facebook page.
4. **Proof, not promises:** free pilot measured against your own baseline, money back if returns do not drop.
